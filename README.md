# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural performance across different seasons using
data analytics techniques.

The analysis investigates seasonal differences in agricultural performance,
environmental conditions, resource usage, economic outcomes, regional patterns,
and unusual observations.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze agricultural performance across seasons
- Identify major seasonal patterns
- Compare environmental conditions
- Analyze crop choices and irrigation methods
- Study resource usage
- Examine relationships between environmental conditions and yield
- Compare economic outcomes
- Analyze regional seasonal patterns
- Identify unusual observations
- Develop evidence-based recommendations

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains agricultural information related to:

- Farm and geographical information
- Crop and season
- Environmental conditions
- Soil characteristics
- Irrigation
- Fertilizer and pesticide usage
- Yield and production
- Revenue, cost and profit
- Water usage and efficiency
- Disease and pest risk

## 🔍 Analytical Questions

1. Which season shows the best agricultural performance based on average yield,
   production, and profit?

2. What major differences in agricultural performance can be observed among
   the seasons?

3. How do environmental conditions such as rainfall, temperature, humidity,
   and sunlight differ across seasons?

4. How do crop choices and irrigation methods vary across different seasons?

5. How does the use of water, fertilizer, and pesticides vary across seasons?

6. Are there relationships between seasonal environmental conditions and
   agricultural performance?

7. How do economic outcomes vary across seasons?

8. Are some seasonal patterns consistent across different regions or categories?

9. Are there unusual or unexpected seasonal patterns?

10. What insights can be derived from the observed seasonal differences?

11. What conclusions can reasonably be drawn from the available data?

12. How could the findings support better seasonal agricultural planning?

## 📈 Key Findings

- Kharif showed the highest average yield, production and profit.
- Zaid showed the lowest average yield and production.
- Zaid had negative average profit in the analyzed dataset.
- Environmental conditions varied considerably across seasons.
- Zaid had the highest average water usage but the lowest water efficiency.
- Seasonal performance varied across different states.
- Several unusual yield observations were identified.

## 📌 Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that agricultural performance varies across seasons
and regions. Seasonal planning should consider environmental conditions,
resource efficiency, crop selection, economic performance and regional
characteristics.
